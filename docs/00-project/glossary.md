# Project Glossary (English • Français • العربية)

To guarantee absolute consistency across product architecture, UI copywriting, and engineering specifications, this glossary standardizes domain vocabulary across English, French, and Algerian Arabic.

| Domain Term (English) | Terme (Français) | المصطلح (العربية) | Definition & Contextual Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OFOQ** | OFOQ | أفق | The digital ecosystem connecting children, families, and professionals. Translates to "Horizon", symbolizing individualized potential. |
| **Family Account** | Compte Famille | حساب العائلة | The parent-owned master account that manages subscription, settings, and one or more Child Profiles. |
| **Parent** | Parent / Tuteur | الولي / الوالد(ة) | The authenticated account holder with legal authority to manage children's profiles, grant professional access, and configure preferences. |
| **Professional / Pro** | Professionnel (Pro) | أخصائي / مهني | An authenticated practitioner (speech therapist, educator, psychomotor therapist) authorized by a parent to access specific child data. |
| **Admin** | Administrateur | مسؤول المنصة | Platform governance account responsible for verifying professionals, moderating shared libraries, and auditing platform security. |
| **Child Profile** | Profil Enfant | ملف الطفل | The administrative entity representing a child within a family account. Contains age, preferences, goals, and assigned specialists. |
| **Child World** | Monde de l'Enfant | عالم الطفل | The personalized, autism-friendly interactive workspace nested within the parent account for a specific child. (Not a separate login role). |
| **AAC** | CAA (Communication Améliorée et Alternative) | التواصل المعزز والبديل | Augmentative and Alternative Communication; symbol-based, visual, or speech-generating tools supporting non-verbal or minimally verbal individuals. |
| **Pictogram** | Pictogramme | صورة رمزية / بيكتوغرام | Standardized, high-clarity visual symbol representing a concept, object, feeling, action, or need. |
| **Communication Card** | Carte de Communication | بطاقة تواصل | An interactive AAC element combining a pictogram, written text, audio recording, and category classification. |
| **Professional Assignment** | Affectation Professionnelle | تخصيص / تكليف الأخصائي | The formal, parent-authorized relationship binding an authenticated professional to a specific child profile. |
| **Consent** | Consentement Éclairé | موافقة مسبقة | Affirmative legal agreement by the parent authorizing data collection, processing, or third-party professional sharing under Algerian Law 18-07. |
| **Permission Grant** | Attribution d'Autorisation | منح الصلاحية | Granular authorization granted by a parent to a professional (e.g., read progress, assign activities, edit vocabulary). Revocable at any time. |
| **Progress Event** | Événement de Progrès | حدث تقدم | An empirical log entry capturing a completed activity, AAC communication exchange, or milestone reached by the child. |
| **Observation** | Observation Clinique / Pratique | ملاحظة تتبعية | Structured clinical or educational note recorded by an authorized professional or parent documenting child engagement or behavior. |
| **AI Companion** | Compagnon IA | رفيق الذكاء الاصطناعي | Optional, parent-activated assistive AI with an individualized persona and voice. Strictly non-clinical, non-diagnostic, and isolated per child. |
| **OFOQ Academy** | Académie OFOQ | أكاديمية أفق | Curated repository of educational articles, video tutorials, and guides for parents and professionals. |
| **Non-Diagnostic Boundary** | Limite Non-Diagnostique | حد غير تشخيصي | Strict product principle: OFOQ does not evaluate, score, diagnose, or prescribe medical treatments for autism or speech disorders. |
| **Content Provenance** | Traçabilité du Contenu | مصدر ومصداقية المحتوى | Explicit tracking of whether an activity or card originated from a Professional, Parent, or OFOQ Intelligence. |
