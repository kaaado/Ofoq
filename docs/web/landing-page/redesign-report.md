## 1. Brand Identity Overview

**OFOQ (أفق)** is an Algerian digital ecosystem connecting children with autism and communication difficulties, their parents, and licensed professionals.

### 1.1 Brand Personality & Tone
- **Dignity & Care:** Respects the autonomy and pace of neurodivergent children.
- **Professional Trust:** Reassures speech therapists, physicians, and educators with solid clinical boundaries.
- **Warmth & Family Hope:** Creates an inviting, stigma-free atmosphere for parents navigating daily challenges.
- **Sensory Calm:** Zero aggressive neon colors, zero flashing banners, gentle animations, and glare-free backgrounds.

## 2. Refined Brand Color Palette

The official color palette is composed of five distinct brand colors and one neutral canvas:

| Color Name | Hex Code | Role | Purpose & Usage |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |

| **Muted Coral** | `#D58C7A` | **Human Accent** | Warmth, empathy, family connection, AAC tactile action cards. |
| **Porcelain** | `#F5F6F3` | **Canvas** | Neutral foundation; replaces clinical stark white with a warm, glare-free background. |

## 3. The 3D Spacetoon Child World Metaphor

Inspired by the cultural heritage of Spacetoon's themed planets, OFOQ models each child profile as a personalized, 3D celestial **"Child World"**:

1. **Profile Generation:** When a parent creates a child profile in the Family Account, a unique planet is instantiated.
2. **Planet Themes:**
   - **Planet of Sounds (كوكب الأصوات / Planète des Sons):** Focused on phonetics, speech stimulation, and vocal play.
   - **Planet of Wonders (كوكب العجائب / Planète des Merveilles):** Visual storytelling, sensory exploration, and emotional expression.
   - **Planet of Calm (كوكب الهدوء / Planète Douceur):** Soothing sensory breaks, quiet timers, and routine stabilization.
3. **Interactive Components:**
   - Floating celestial rings and orbiting moons representing therapy goals.
   - Tactile pictogram stars that speak aloud upon touch.
   - Zero-password child-lock switch to prevent exiting without parental PIN.

## 4. Typography Hierarchy

- **Arabic Scripts:**
  - Headings: **Alexandria** (geometric, modern, dignified Arabic letterforms).
  - Body & UI: **Tajawal** (clear, legible at small font sizes, high legibility for neurodivergent readers).
- **Latin Scripts (French & English):**
  - Headings & Body: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (clean, contemporary humanist sans-serif).
